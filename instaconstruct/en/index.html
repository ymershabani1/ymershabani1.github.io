Our Process

From workshop to your doorstep

Every Instaconstruct home follows a carefully orchestrated journey — from factory-built modules, through precision delivery and installation, to a turnkey home ready for life.

01

Module Transport

Prefabricated modules are loaded onto trucks and delivered directly to your site, arriving ready for assembly within hours.

02

On-site Installation

Our team installs and connects the modules with precision engineering, weatherproofing every joint and detail along the way.

03

Your Finished Home

What started as a workshop module becomes a fully functional, beautifully finished home — handed over turnkey and ready to move in.

Construction

Sample Wall Systems

Transparent layer build-ups for foundation, exterior and interior walls, floor slabs, and roof — precisely engineered for insulation, structure, and services.

Interiors

Kitchen models & prefabricated bathrooms

Interiors are planned with the timber shell — choose from our kitchen models or a fully custom kitchen, alongside prefabricated bathroom modules engineered off-site and delivered ready to connect.

Kitchen models & custom kitchens

We supply curated kitchen models and bespoke layouts: workflow, storage, daylight, cabinetry, appliances, and services are coordinated with the modular structure so installation stays efficient and the finish feels cohesive.

Prefabricated bathrooms

These are not conventional on-site wet rooms alone: our prefabricated bathroom modules are built with integrated services in the workshop, then delivered and connected on the construction site — faster, controlled quality, and a clear match to modular construction.

Premium factory-finished module

Fully fitted bathroom in a transport frame — marble, glass shower, and integrated services, ready to connect on site.
