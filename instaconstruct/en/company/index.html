Who We Are

Who We Are

Instaconstruct is an innovative company in modular wood construction. We combine decades of construction expertise with modern manufacturing technology to create homes that unite aesthetics, sustainability, and efficiency. Our experienced team guides every client from the first design to the turnkey handover.

Our Values

Sustainability

We rely on certified timber from responsible forestry and consistently minimize our ecological footprint.

Quality

Precision-manufactured modules, strict quality controls, and premium materials ensure the highest construction standards.

Innovation

Continuous development of our design solutions to make the living dreams of the future achievable today.

Partnership

Long-term relationships with clients, suppliers, and partners form the foundation of our success.

Mission

Provide specialized modular construction for high-value projects by adhering to strict environmental norms and building practices.
